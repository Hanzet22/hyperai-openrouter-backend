⚡ Welcome to HyperAI

HyperAI is a development-stage chat interface that routes requests through OpenRouter and third-party model providers. Availability, rate limits, and data handling may vary by provider.

Provider eligibility

The OpenRouter integration must remain unavailable until an authorized adult operator has reviewed provider account and end-user requirements and approved an eligible access model. Do not misrepresent age or use another person's account.

🛡️ Privacy basics

  • Never submit passwords, API keys, access tokens, identity documents, precise location, or confidential information.
  • Requests may be processed by OpenRouter and the selected model provider under their respective policies.
  • Do not assume chat content is private, permanently stored, or automatically deleted.
  • Use synthetic or redacted data for debugging and examples.

This is a development build. Privacy and terms documents remain drafts and are not legal advice or a compliance certification.

Privasi & penyimpanan

HyperAI menyimpan preferensi tema, motion, model, dan mode berpikir di penyimpanan lokal browser. Isi percakapan hanya berada di memori halaman saat ini dan tidak dibuat menjadi riwayat persisten oleh antarmuka ini. Request yang dikirim ke provider tetap tunduk pada kebijakan provider masing-masing.

Jangan kirim API key, kata sandi, token, dokumen identitas, atau data rahasia.
